Limited use

Your draft is not a product.

Typed English is sent only to generate a correction. Zaixos does not sell it, does not use it for ads, and does not train a public model on your private drafts.

Contact support@zaixos.com or hello@zaixos.com.

What leaves the browser

After you agree in the popup, the current writing segment — usually the sentence you just typed, never a password field — is sent over HTTPS to writing-api.zaixos.com, then to Groq for structured correction.

Stored on your machine

On/off, highlights, and up to 20 recent pairs. Clear them anytime.

Not stored as your diary

Server logs keep request id, timing, and status — not the full draft.
