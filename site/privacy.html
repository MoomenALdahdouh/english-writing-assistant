Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Zaixos (“we”, “us”) handles information in the English Writing Assistant Chrome extension and the related site at writing.zaixos.com. It is written to satisfy the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including Limited Use.

Contact: support@zaixos.com · hello@zaixos.com

1. Single purpose

The product has one purpose: suggest English spelling, grammar, punctuation, and obvious wording corrections while you type in supported fields on websites. We do not use data collected for this purpose for advertising, credit decisions, or unrelated profiling.

2. Information we handle

The extension may handle:

  • User-generated writing. After you agree in the extension popup, the current English text segment (typically up to 2,000 characters of what you are writing now), plus a short optional previous-text snippet and the field type (textarea, text, or contenteditable).
  • Local settings. Whether the assistant is active, whether highlights are on, your consent choice, and the API URL. Stored with chrome.storage.sync.
  • Local history. Up to 20 recent original/corrected pairs on your device via chrome.storage.local. You can clear this from the popup.
  • Technical request metadata on our API: time, request id, model name, latency, HTTP status, and token counts. We do not keep the full draft in ordinary server logs.

We do not intentionally collect passwords, payment details, or browsing history.

3. What we do not access

The content script ignores password fields and common non-text inputs, and it skips known code editors (Monaco, CodeMirror, Ace). It does not read cookies, tabs APIs, or your Google account.

4. Why we handle this information

Correction requires a language model. The browser package cannot safely hold the model API key, so text is sent over HTTPS to https://writing-api.zaixos.com and then to Groq to produce a structured correction. That is the only reason text leaves your browser.

5. Sharing

We share the correction payload with Groq as our processing provider. Groq’s handling is also governed by Groq’s terms and privacy policy. We do not sell your writing. We do not share it with advertisers. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. Limited Use disclosure

User data obtained through this extension is used only to provide and improve the disclosed single purpose: English writing correction. We do not use it to determine creditworthiness, and we do not transfer it for unrelated commercial purposes. This statement is our Chrome Web Store Limited Use disclosure.

7. Consent

No writing is sent until you click I agree and continue in the extension popup. You can stop sending data by pausing the extension or uninstalling it.

8. Security and retention

Transmissions use HTTPS. Settings and history stay in Chrome storage on your profile. API logs are operational (ids, timing, status) and are not a backup of your documents. Groq retains data according to its own policy. We do not keep a permanent archive of your drafts on Zaixos servers.

9. Your choices

  • Pause the extension — no correction requests are sent.
  • Clear history in the popup — local pairs are deleted.
  • Uninstall — the content script and local storage for this item are removed.
  • Email support@zaixos.com to ask what we hold or to request deletion of any server logs we can identify.

10. Children

The product is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their personal information.

11. Permissions

  • storage — save settings and local history.
  • Host permission for writing-api.zaixos.com — send correction requests over HTTPS.
  • Content scripts on http/https pages — detect supported writing fields and show the inline correction row. This is required for a writing assistant that works where you type.

12. Changes

If our practices change, we will update this page and the effective date. Material changes that affect how writing is handled will also be reflected in the in-product disclosure.
