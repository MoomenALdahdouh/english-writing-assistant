# Privacy

The public policy for Chrome Web Store review is:

**https://writing.zaixos.com/privacy**

This file is a short developer summary. The website policy is the source of truth.

- After in-popup consent, English text segments are sent over HTTPS to `https://writing-api.zaixos.com`, then to Groq.
- Settings and up to 20 history pairs stay in Chrome storage.
- Server logs do not keep full drafts.
- Contact: support@zaixos.com and hello@zaixos.com.
